SwitchBoard

SYSTEM 01 · LLM GATEWAY

SwitchBoard

MULTI-PROVIDER ROUTING · FAILOVER · QUOTA

One endpoint in front of OpenAI, Gemini and Groq. Requests route by cost and health. When a provider degrades, the fallback chain takes over and the application layer never finds out.

Retries, quota accounting and centralised logging live in the gateway, so swapping a provider is a config change rather than a refactor.

FIG. 1LANE OCCUPANCY · SCHEMATIC

Fig. 1 Traffic hops lanes twice; the caller's response code never changes.

STACKPython · FastAPI · Redis
ROLEDesign and build

PharmaGuard

SYSTEM 02 · CLINICAL DECISION SUPPORT

PharmaGuard

PHARMACOGENOMICS · CPIC · VCF

Genomic VCF in, CPIC-guided prescribing guidance out. Every clinical call is made by a deterministic risk engine. The model gets no vote.

The LLM sits on the far side of a hard line, doing one job: turning a decided recommendation into prose a clinician will read. If it fails you lose the explanation, not the recommendation.

FIG. 2RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARY

Fig. 2 One arrow crosses the line, one direction only. That constraint is the architecture.

INPUTVCF → PharmCAT diplotypes
DECIDESRule engine over CPIC tables
EXPLAINSLlama 3.3, constrained
OUTFastAPI → EHR-shaped JSON

MediAssist AI

VOICE + CHAT INTAKE · RAG DASHBOARD

End-to-end appointments over chat and voice. LLM workflows handle patient identification and intake; the doctor gets a dashboard with RAG assistance over prior records.

The patient never fills a form and the doctor never waits on a summary.

SYSTEM 04 · NUMPY ONLY

Dropout, from scratch

NO FRAMEWORK · PAPER REPRODUCTION

A complete network in Python and NumPy — forward pass, backprop, and dropout reimplemented from the paper instead of imported.

The deliverable was never accuracy. It was reproducing the figure: watching validation loss peel away from training loss without dropout, and stay pinned to it with.

FIG. 4TRAIN / VALIDATION LOSS

Fig. 4 The textbook shape, reproduced by hand. Illustrative of the run's behaviour, not a published benchmark.

BUILTLayers, backprop, dropout
DEPSPython, NumPy. Nothing else.

WHAT THIS ISN'T

Limits

Most of what's here is hackathon- or coursework-scale. SwitchBoard has served real traffic. PharmaGuard has not been clinically validated and shouldn't be read as if it had.

I'm strongest from the model boundary outward — routing, retrieval, evaluation, deciding what a model is permitted to touch. I have not trained anything at scale, and I'd rather write that down than let a résumé imply otherwise.

STRONGGateways, failover, retrieval, evals
