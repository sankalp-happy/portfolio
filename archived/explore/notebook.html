01 · LLM GATEWAY

SwitchBoard

One endpoint in front of OpenAI, Gemini and Groq. Routes on cost and health, retries, accounts for quota, logs centrally. When a provider degrades the chain shifts and the application layer never finds out.

02 · CLINICAL DECISION SUPPORT

PharmaGuard

Genomic VCF in, CPIC-guided prescribing guidance out. A deterministic risk engine makes every clinical call. The model gets no vote — it is confined to writing the explanation a clinician reads, downstream of a decision already made.

03 · 1ST PLACE, STATE-LEVEL HACKATHON

MediAssist AI

Appointments over chat and voice. LLM workflows do patient identification and intake; the doctor gets a dashboard with RAG-backed assistance over prior records. The patient never fills a form and the doctor never waits for a summary.

04 · NUMPY ONLY

Dropout, from scratch

A complete network in Python and NumPy — forward, backward, and dropout reimplemented from the paper instead of imported. The deliverable wasn't accuracy. It was reproducing the figure.

In [5]:
def dropout(a, p, training): if not training: return a mask = (rng.random(a.shape) > p) return a * mask / (1.0 - p) # inverted: test-time is a no-op

06 · WHAT THIS ISN'T

Limits

Most of the above is hackathon- or coursework-scale. SwitchBoard has served real traffic. PharmaGuard has not been clinically validated and shouldn't be read as if it had.

I'm strongest from the model boundary outward — routing, retrieval, evaluation, deciding what a model is permitted to touch. I have not trained anything at scale. I'd rather write that down than let a résumé imply otherwise.
